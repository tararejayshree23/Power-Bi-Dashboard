# Power-Bi-Dashboard

**Motorola Mobile Sales Dashboard**
 **Overview**
This dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of Motorola mobile sales across India. It integrates multiple dimensions such as geography, time, brand, payment methods, and customer ratings to deliver actionable business insights.

The dashboard is designed for business analysts, sales managers, and decision-makers to monitor performance, identify trends, and optimize strategies.

**Key Metrics**
Total Sales: ₹105M
Total Quantity Sold: 3K units
Transactions: 544
Average Transaction Value: ₹40K

**Sales by Geography**
Highest sales observed in major metro cities including Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, and Kolkata.

Smaller cities like Ludhiana, Patna, and Ranchi also contribute significantly, indicating widespread demand across regions.

**Monthly Trends**
Peak sales in November (259 units) and July (248 units).
Lowest sales in April (163 units).
Seasonal fluctuations suggest festive season boosts and mid-year promotions drive higher sales.

**Payment Method Analysis**
Credit Card: 33.83% (most preferred)
Debit Card: 24.81%
UPI: 24.06%
Cash: 17.29%
Insight: Digital payments dominate, highlighting the need for strong online transaction infrastructure.

**Mobile Model Insights**
Vivo Y51: ₹9.6M
OnePlus 9: ₹8.9M
iPhone SE: ₹8.1M
These models are key revenue drivers and should be prioritized in marketing campaigns.

**Customer Ratings**
Mixed ratings with some strong positive feedback but also notable dissatisfaction.

Action: Focus on after-sales service and customer support to improve retention.

**Day-wise Sales**
Wednesday: ₹105M (highest sales concentration).

Suggests mid-week promotions may be highly effective.

**Business Insights**
Festive & seasonal promotions significantly boost sales (November peak).

Digital payments dominate, requiring robust online transaction systems.

OnePlus and Vivo outperform competitors, indicating strong consumer preference.

Geographic spread shows both metro and tier-2 cities are important markets.

Customer satisfaction needs improvement to sustain long-term growth.

**Usage**
This dashboard can be used to:

Track monthly and city-level performance.

Compare brand and model sales.

Analyze payment preferences.

Identify customer experience gaps.

Support strategic decision-making in sales and marketing.
